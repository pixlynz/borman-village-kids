Editing your website

The main words and photos on your website can be changed from your WordPress dashboard, without touching any code. This guide walks you through it.

Logging in

Go to bormanvillagekids.co.nz/wp-admin and log in with your WordPress.com email and password. You'll land on the dashboard, with a menu down the left side.

Tip: while logged in and browsing your own site, a dark admin bar runs across the top. "Edit Page" in that bar jumps you straight to editing the page you're looking at.

About the "Home (page content)" page: your actual homepage isn't a page you can open and edit directly, it's built from this one. Open Pages → Home (page content) to change the homepage's words and photos; your visitors see the real homepage, built using whatever you've entered there.

Changing words on a page

  1. Go to Pages and click the page you want, for example About Us.
  2. Scroll down past the usual title/content area to the Page content box. Each editable piece of text or photo on that page has its own field, labelled to match what it is (for example "Hero photo" or "'Family focus' paragraph").
  3. Click into a text box and type your changes.
  4. To replace a photo, click the photo field and choose "Remove" then upload a new one, or use "Edit Image" if one's already there.
  5. Click the blue Update button on the right to save.
  6. Click View Page to check how it looks on the live site.

A few things to know:

  • Leave a blank line between paragraphs if a field holds more than one.
  • Photos look best close to the same shape (wide or square) as the one you're replacing — a very differently shaped photo may get cropped oddly.
  • If you leave a field blank, the site just falls back to showing the original text or photo, nothing breaks.

What you can't change here

To keep the design and layout working properly, a few things are fixed in the site's code rather than editable fields:

  • Navigation menu, buttons, and small labels (like "Kai", section kickers)
  • Contact details, opening hours, and social media links in the footer
  • The "Enquire" and "Enrolment application" links, which go to your Discover enrolment system
  • Colours, fonts, and overall page layout
