Editing your website

The main words and photos on your website can be changed from your WordPress dashboard, without touching any code. This guide walks you through it.

Getting started

Logging in

Go to bormanvillagekids.co.nz/wp-admin and log in with your WordPress.com email and password. You'll land on the dashboard, with a menu down the left side.

While logged in and browsing your own site, a dark admin bar runs across the top. Edit Page in that bar jumps you straight to editing the page you're looking at.

How to

Changing words on a page

1

Open the page

Go to Pages, click the page you want (e.g. About Us).

2

Find the content box

Scroll to the Page content box — each field is labelled to match what it is.

3

Make your changes

Type into a text box, or click a photo field to swap the image.

4

Save and check

Click Update, then View Page to see it live.

Leave a blank line between paragraphs if a field holds more than one. Photos look best close to the same shape as the one you're replacing. Leaving a field blank just shows the original text or photo — nothing breaks.
Good to know

What you can't change here

A few things stay fixed in the site's code to protect the design:

  • Navigation menu, buttons, and small labels
  • Contact details, hours, and social links in the footer
  • The "Enquire" and "Enrolment application" links (your Discover system)
  • Colours, fonts, and overall page layout
Need any of these changed? Get in touch with Michelle at Pixly and she'll make the update for you.
