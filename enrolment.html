Fees & funding

Support to make it work.

We want quality early education to be within reach. A couple of options can help with the cost of care.

  • 20 Hours ECEAvailable for eligible children, helping with the cost of your weekly hours.
  • WINZ subsidiesWork and Income childcare subsidies are available for families who qualify.
  • Securing a future startFor enrolments starting later, the first week's fees may be required to hold your place.
